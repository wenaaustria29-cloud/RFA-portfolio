Before we begin

Common questions.

What is the turnaround time?

I confirm the timeline after reviewing your scope, transaction volume and the condition of your records. We agree on deliverables and deadlines before work begins.

How will software access be arranged?

Please invite me through your accounting software’s user-access settings, with permissions limited to the agreed work. Password sharing is not needed.

How is my data handled?

I never share passwords, names, account numbers or other identifying details with any outside tool or service. Confidential files are shared only through channels we agree on, and I'm happy to sign an NDA before we start.

What does a typical month include?

A monthly scope may include recording and categorizing transactions, reconciling accounts, tracking invoices and bills, and preparing financial reports. The final scope depends on your business and is agreed in advance.

How do I request a quote?

Send me the software you use, transaction volume, required services and any backlog. I will review the scope and provide a tailored quote before we start.

What do your packages cost?

Packages start at USD 300 a month for a small business, USD 500 for a growing business, and USD 12 an hour for catch-up or flexible support. The final price is quoted after I review your scope. I suggest a one-month trial before a longer arrangement.

Do you provide tax, audit or legal advice?

No. I provide bookkeeping and accounting support. For audit opinions, legal advice or licensed tax advice, you would work with a qualified professional, and I can supply organized records to support them.

What hours are you available?

I am based in the Philippines (UTC+8) and open to Australian daytime, UAE/Dubai hours, or US-shift arrangements. We can confirm the required overlap and start date when discussing the role.

Not sure where to start?

Tell me about your business and where your books stand. I'll suggest a starting point and send you a quote.
